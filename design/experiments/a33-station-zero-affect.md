# A3-3 — Station Zero affect expression

## Upstream

Ordivon Studio revision `7796af0ee0a30c4cf166b98354c200b62699b86a` owns the cross-medium A3-3 experiment (`research/expression/experiments/a33-station-zero-affect.md`). The source is the **unregistered Station Zero v3 replacement target**, not the registered v2 product. The Web candidate uses the same frozen Station Zero v3 Genesis but stays inside the Rescue player knowledge envelope.

## Web hypothesis

The desired affect is **isolation + latent dread + fragile hope**. Web should produce that state primarily through spatial proportion and pause rather than hidden enemy art, animation, or warning language.

The main composition therefore contains:

- one very small known island with three discovered rooms;
- three confirmed healthy specialists as the strongest human anchor;
- a much larger unresolved field that is explicitly **unknown, not empty World state**;
- a non-localized `unknown-life-signs` report;
- frozen Genesis telemetry rather than trend lines;
- a compact mandatory Rescue objective that prevents the scene becoming nihilistic.

## Source discipline

The Web expression must not show exact Pirate/Swarm positions, hidden hazards, unknown systems, fabricated movement, or time trends. The public page may explain the experimental boundary, but the experiential field itself remains Rescue-near.

## Falsifiers

Revise if:

- negative space looks like missing content rather than bounded unknown;
- the map island becomes too large and loses the intended scale contrast;
- the unknown-life-signs report looks spatially localized;
- frozen telemetry reads as a countdown or live trend;
- the mandatory Rescue objective becomes louder than the unknown field;
- mobile collapses the composition into an ordinary vertical dashboard;
- the treatment claims a population actually feels dread/isolation without response evidence.


## First composition correction

The first layout put `unknown-life-signs` on the right side of the affect field. Although no connector was drawn, the position itself could be read as a bearing. The report was moved into a wide non-map HUD band spanning the unresolved field.

> Non-localized information can be accidentally localized by composition alone.


## Rendered observation

The real static Game project page was captured at 1440px and 390px. The known Rescue island occupies about `22.8%` of the affect field on desktop and `29.2%` on mobile, while preserving three rooms and three confirmed contacts with no horizontal overflow.

The first report layout was rejected because right-side placement could imply a bearing. The corrected non-map report band spans about `62.5%` of the desktop field and `91.3%` on mobile, while explicitly retaining no position/bearing in Rescue knowledge.

## Decision

**Promote only as a bounded expression of the unregistered Station Zero v3 target.**

The section may enter the Game project page because it:

1. labels v3 as an unregistered target rather than current product truth;
2. uses an exact frozen Genesis and the Rescue knowledge envelope;
3. exposes no hidden Pirate/Swarm position or hidden hazard;
4. keeps telemetry static rather than inventing trends;
5. preserves a materially larger unresolved field on desktop and mobile;
6. corrected a compositional side channel where position could imply bearing;
7. makes no population claim that readers actually feel dread, isolation, or hope.

The existing project mechanism and product-state copy remain the inspection/authority layer beneath the affective pause.
