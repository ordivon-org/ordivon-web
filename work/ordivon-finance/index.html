Personal finance · Observable account state · Human authority

Understand a changing account before allowing an Agent to act.

Ordivon Finance connects personal account evidence to current state, changes, uncertainty, Agent explanations, bounded proposals, human review, paper execution, and later reconciliation. Its purpose is not to predict wealth. It is to make one capital decision loop usable, inspectable, and recoverable.

Central question

Can one person see what they own, what changed, what is uncertain, what the Agent proposes, and what actually happened?

Conventional tools often separate account exports, portfolio views, research, proposed action, order submission, and retrospective review. Ordivon Finance joins those stages through one observable state and consequence-aware loop without allowing a fluent explanation or readiness flag to become capital authority.

The current proving environment is an external paper investment account. Funded trading, transfers, custody, tax submission, and autonomous capital management remain outside the product boundary.

Next evidence

Can current governance terminology disappear behind one understandable local interface while preserving the consequence boundary?
